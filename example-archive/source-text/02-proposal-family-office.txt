PROPOSAL — Reputation Programme
Client: Halvorsen Family Office (fictional)
Date: 2 June 2026    Ref: MP-2026-031

SCOPE
A six-month retained reputation programme covering principal profile management, third-party
commentary monitoring, and preparation for two anticipated transactions.

FEES
Monthly retainer of USD 14,500 covering up to 40 advisory hours. Hours beyond 40 in any
month are billed at the standard senior counsel rate in rate card RC-2026. Crisis
mobilisation outside business hours carries a 50 percent uplift.

TERMINATION
Either party may terminate on 60 days written notice. Fees are payable to the end of the
notice period. Work product created before termination remains licensed to the client.

EXCLUSIONS
Litigation support, regulatory filings, and any engagement that would place the adviser in
contact with the counterparties to the anticipated transactions.
