CLAUSE 7 — LIABILITY
The Adviser's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid
in the twelve months preceding the claim. This cap is a material term and the fee structure
assumes it. Do not accept an uncapped liability clause, and do not accept a cap expressed as
a multiple of fees without repricing the engagement.

CLAUSE 9 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Methodologies, frameworks and tools developed by the Adviser before or outside the engagement
remain the Adviser's property. The client receives a perpetual licence to use deliverables
internally. Client-specific work product transfers on final payment, not on delivery.

CLAUSE 12 — CHANGE CONTROL
Any request that alters the scope stated in the applicable Statement of Work requires a
written change order signed by both parties before the work begins. Work performed without a
signed change order is performed at the Adviser's risk and is not billable.

CLAUSE 15 — PAYMENT
Invoices are payable within 30 days. Interest accrues at 4 percent above base after 45 days.
