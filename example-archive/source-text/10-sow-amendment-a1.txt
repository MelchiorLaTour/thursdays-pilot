CHANGE ORDER A1 to SOW-2026-031-A
Client: Halvorsen Family Office (fictional)
Signed: 19 August 2026

REASON
Between 14 July and 8 August the client made four requests that fall outside the scope of
SOW-2026-031-A: two drafts of a transaction announcement, one briefing with a counterparty's
adviser, and one review of a regulatory submission.

DISPOSITION
The two announcement drafts and the regulatory review are added to scope. The counterparty
briefing is refused and remains excluded.

ADDITIONAL FEE

retainer.

NOTE
Three of these four requests were performed before this change order was signed and were
therefore at the Adviser's risk. That is the second time this has happened on this account.
