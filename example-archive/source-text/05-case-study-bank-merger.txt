CASE STUDY — Bank Merger Stakeholder Programme
Client: Aldgate Bank (fictional)    Engagement: February to July 2025

SITUATION
A merger of two retail banks with overlapping branch networks and two incompatible
management cultures. The announced synergy case depended on branch closures nobody had
yet told the regional directors about.

WHAT WE DID
Stakeholder mapping across 41 named individuals using the influence-versus-alignment method.
Identified three regional directors with high informal influence and negative alignment who
did not appear in the sponsor's own list. Built a sequenced briefing programme that reached
them before the closure announcement rather than after.

RESULT
The closure announcement drew no regional-director public dissent. Two of the three later
became programme advocates.

LESSON
Informal power does not appear on an org chart. It appears in who other people mention
unprompted in interviews.
