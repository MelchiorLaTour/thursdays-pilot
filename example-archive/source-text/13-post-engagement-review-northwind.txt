POST-ENGAGEMENT REVIEW — Northwind Air
Written 12 September 2025

WHAT WORKED
Single approver. One channel. Refusing the two interview requests.

WHAT DID NOT
Billing. The engagement ran 62 hours over the estimate because crisis hours were not
separated from advisory hours in the original proposal. I absorbed 41 of those hours because
I had not established the crisis rate before the crisis. Rate card RC-2026 now separates
them. That change came from this engagement.

COLLECTIONS
Final invoice paid at day 71 against 30-day terms. No interest charged, because I did not
want to raise it during the relationship. That is a decision I keep making and should stop.

REUSABLE
The escalation ladder structure. The holding-statement revision discipline.
