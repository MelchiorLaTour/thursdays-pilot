PROPOSAL — Communications Strategy Review
Client: Meridian Sovereign Investment Authority (fictional)
Date: 14 March 2026    Ref: MP-2026-014

SCOPE
A twelve-week review of external communications across the Authority's direct-investment
portfolio, delivering a positioning framework, a spokesperson protocol, and a crisis
escalation ladder.

APPROACH
Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): stakeholder mapping across 22 named counterparties, using the
influence-versus-alignment method described in our Stakeholder Mapping methodology note.
Phase 2 (weeks 4-8): message architecture and testing with six internal reviewers.
Phase 3 (weeks 9-12): crisis escalation ladder and spokesperson protocol.

FEES
Fixed fee of USD 96,000, invoiced in three equal instalments at phase gates.
Expenses billed at cost, capped at 8 percent of fees. Rate card reference RC-2026.

ASSUMPTIONS
The Authority provides a single named counterpart with authority to approve message
architecture. Delay in that approval moves the phase gate and the invoice with it.

EXCLUSIONS
Media buying, paid placement, investor-relations disclosure drafting, and any regulated
financial promotion are explicitly out of scope.
