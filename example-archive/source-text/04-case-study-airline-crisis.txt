CASE STUDY — Airline Operational Crisis
Client: Northwind Air (fictional)    Engagement: August 2025

SITUATION
A four-day operational failure grounded 340 flights. Media coverage moved from operational
to governance within 36 hours, which is the transition that determines whether a crisis ends
in an apology or a resignation.

WHAT WE DID
Stood up a single-channel escalation ladder with three named spokespeople and one approver.
Rewrote the holding statement four times in the first eight hours as facts firmed. Refused
two interview requests that would have put an unbriefed executive on camera.

RESULT
Coverage returned to operational framing by day three. No governance-level departures.

WHAT WE WOULD DO DIFFERENTLY
The escalation ladder was built during the crisis. It should have existed beforehand. Every
retained client now gets one in the first 30 days as a standing deliverable.
